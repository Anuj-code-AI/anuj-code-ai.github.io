July – August 2026

Distributed Job Scheduler

A fault-tolerant scheduler that runs asynchronous jobs across horizontally scaled workers.

  • Atomic database-level job claiming, so two workers never take the same job.
  • Exponential backoff retries, plus a dead-letter queue for jobs that keep failing.
  • Resilience4j circuit breaker and retry with fallback logic, so a Redis outage doesn't lose jobs.
  • APIs to submit, cancel, and track jobs, monitored with Prometheus and Grafana.
Spring BootRedisResilience4jEurekaPrometheusGrafana
